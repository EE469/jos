e)
The transition occurs after the jump to $relocated. In kern/entrypgdir.c, both the virtual address ranges [0, 4MB) and [KERNBASE, KERNBASE+4MB) are mapped to the physical address range [0, 4MB). This mapping is essential because the rest of the kernel is placed at high addresses in kern_pgdir, and once kern_pgdir is loaded, the virtual address range [0, 4MB) no longer corresponds to [0, 4MB). Additionally, [0, 4MB) is not specifically allocated for the kernel; rather, it is used temporarily before paging is enabled, as higher addresses like 0xf0000000 are not accessible at that stage.
